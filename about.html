Who We Are

Lystra Foundation Uganda is a community-based organization established on the principles of integrated community development. Our work is driven by the belief that sustainable transformation requires a holistic approach that addresses the social, economic, environmental, and developmental needs of communities.

We work to strengthen community capacities, promote inclusion, create opportunities for vulnerable and underserved groups, and support locally driven solutions to development challenges.

Our Vision

A sustainable, inclusive, and empowered society.

Our Mission

To empower communities through integrated and inclusive development initiatives that promotes environmental sustainability, effective leadership, quality education, economic empowerment, and access to safe water, sanitation, and hygiene services.

Our Core Values

The foundational principles that guide our everyday work and partnerships.

Integrity

Upholding honesty, transparency, and accountability.

Inclusion

Promoting equal participation and opportunities for all, especially vulnerable and underserved groups.

Community Participation

Ensuring communities play an active role in identifying needs and shaping solutions.

Sustainability

Supporting long-term solutions that benefit both present and future generations.

Empowerment

Building knowledge, skills, confidence, and self-reliance.

Innovation & Partnership

Encouraging creative approaches and working collaboratively to achieve shared development goals.

Our Approach

We believe communities are the drivers of their own development. Our approach emphasizes participation, local ownership, capacity building, inclusion, collaboration, and sustainability. By integrating our five thematic areas, we seek to address interconnected community challenges while building on local strengths, knowledge, and opportunities.
