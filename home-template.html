Lystra Foundation Uganda is a community-based organization committed to integrated community development, environmental protection, education, and sustainable livelihoods.

Welcome to Lystra Foundation Uganda

We believe that sustainable development begins when people are empowered to identify their challenges, develop local solutions and take an active role in transforming their communities.

Through community engagement, capacity building, mentorship, advocacy and strategic partnerships, we strive to build resilient, inclusive and sustainable communities where everyone has an opportunity to thrive.

Our Mission

To empower communities through integrated and inclusive development initiatives that promotes environmental sustainability, effective leadership, quality education, economic empowerment and access to safe water, sanitation and hygiene services.

Our Key Focus Areas

Addressing interconnected community challenges through holistic development programs.

Environmental Protection

Promoting environmental conservation, ecosystem restoration and sustainable natural resource management.

Leadership Development

Nurturing responsible leaders through mentorship, training and personal development.

Education & Child Development

Supporting children's education, protection, well-being, and holistic development.

Entrepreneurship Development

Empowering youth, women, and persons with disabilities (PWDs) with entrepreneurial skills and opportunities for sustainable livelihoods.

Water, Sanitation & Hygiene (WASH)

Promoting safe water, improved sanitation and good hygiene practices for healthier communities.

Be Part of the Change

Together, we can empower people, protect our environment and build stronger communities.
