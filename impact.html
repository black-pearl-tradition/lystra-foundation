Our Impact

Creating lasting change in communities through integrated development.

Creating Lasting Change in Communities

At Lystra Foundation Uganda, we believe that meaningful development is measured by positive changes in people's lives, stronger communities, improved livelihoods and a healthier environment.

Through our integrated community development approach, we aim to create opportunities for individuals and communities to build resilience, develop skills, protect natural resources and improve their well-being.
